# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Learners exploring homelab, networking, and cybersecurity topics, and employers or collaborators reviewing technical projects. The audiences are equally important.

## Product Purpose

JamieLab is a personal technical website for sharing homelab and security experiments, documenting what was learned, showcasing projects, and helping readers troubleshoot practical problems. Success means visitors can learn from real work, understand the projects, and use the notes and guides to solve related tasks.

## Positioning

JamieLab brings practical experiments, project work, and troubleshooting material together in one personal lab site. Its credibility comes from accurately documented work and lessons, not from invented claims or endorsements.

## Operating Context

The site is a Jekyll project hosted on GitHub Pages at jamielab.me. Visitors browse blog posts, project showcases, homelab infrastructure documentation, technical notes, and reference material. The site includes client-side search, an RSS feed, code-copy controls, and local-storage-backed notes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Jekyll with kramdown and Rouge powers the site.
- Content is organized as posts and the `notes`, `projects`, and `info` collections.
- The site supports mobile layouts, accessibility features, print-friendly layouts, five selectable themes, and a dark/light mode toggle.
- The Giscus comments integration is configured but disabled.
- Preserve the current JamieLab name and keep published project, experiment, and technical content accurate.
- The repository is licensed under the MIT License.

## Brand Commitments

The established name is JamieLab. Its published tagline is “Experiments. Networks. Homelab. Code.”

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains published technical posts, project pages, homelab documentation, and reference notes. These are the evidence for the work shown. Do not invent testimonials, customers, certifications, benchmarks, or project outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Ground explanations in practical experiments and documented work.
- Help readers learn and troubleshoot while giving reviewers a clear view of project capability.
- Keep claims about projects and results accurate to the available evidence.
- Preserve accessibility, mobile support, and the usefulness of the site's technical content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing site advertises accessibility features and mobile support. Preserve these capabilities when changing the site.
